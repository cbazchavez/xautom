## 1. Positioning & Unique Angle

### Who You Are (Public Persona)
**Visionary AI entrepreneur who builds, not just talks.** You create AI-native SaaS products and deliver AI/tech solutions for businesses and governments. You're analytical, impact-driven, and curate everything — from business decisions to personal style — with intention.

### Your Differentiator
Unlike most AI creators who either:
- Explain AI conceptually (educators)
- Report AI news (journalists)
- Sell courses (gurus)

**You operate at the intersection of builder + strategist + insider.** You've scaled AI products, worked with governments, and understand both the consumer ChatGPT use case AND enterprise implementation. This gives you credibility across audiences.

### Brand Pillars (Implicit, "Show Don't Tell")
- **Taste & curation** — Visual production, personal style, what you consume (music, food, spaces) communicates quality without explicit lifestyle content
- **Impact orientation** — SDGs 8, 9, 10, 17 woven into stories when relevant, not performative
- **Builder credibility** — Your opinions carry weight because you've shipped products, not just commented on them

### Voice & Tone
- Analytical but accessible (Fer Griego influence)
- Conversational, not corporate (AskCatGPT influence)
- Confident without arrogance
- Spanish-language, clean diction, measured pace

## 4. Reference Creator Analysis

### Fer Griego (@fergriego)
**Category:** Legal/Business Education (Mexico)

#### What makes him effective:
| Element | Observation | Takeaway for Luis |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Hooks** | Provocative, specific, curiosity-driven ("Cómo apoderarte de una empresa sin el consentimiento de sus socios") | Lead with the most dramatic or counterintuitive element |
| **Storytelling** | Walks through scenarios step-by-step with concrete numbers | Use specific numbers and walk through mechanics |
| **Accessibility** | Explains legal/financial concepts without jargon | Translate AI/tech complexity into universal language |
| **Delivery** | Monotone but confident — the script carries the engagement | Script quality > delivery performance |
| **Visual hooks** | Bold text overlay stating the topic immediately | Always pair verbal hook with text hook |
| **CTA pattern** | "Sígueme para más" + content teases | Simple, direct, no overcomplication |

#### Fer Griego Hook Formula:
```
[Provocative outcome/scenario] + [Implied you can do this too]
↓
"Cómo [lograr X controversial/deseable] — te explico"
```

#### Script Structure (Fer Griego):
1. **Hook** (0-3s): State the provocative outcome or scenario
2. **Context** (3-15s): Set up why this matters or how it works broadly
3. **Mechanism** (15-50s): Step-by-step walkthrough with specifics
4. **Implications** (50-55s): What this means / why you should care
5. **CTA** (55-60s): Follow for part 2 / more content

---

### Zauey Talks (@zaueytalks)
**Category:** VC/Tech News & Analysis

#### What makes her effective:
| Element | Observation | Takeaway for Luis |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **News packaging** | Takes complex deals/news and makes them digestible in <90s | Don't assume audience knows context — build it in |
| **Opinion layer** | Adds predictions and "what I think" on top of facts | News alone isn't differentiated — your take is |
| **Series formats** | "Hot Startup Rounds" is repeatable and anticipated | Create signature series that audiences expect weekly |
| **Structure** | Clear sections: what happened → why it matters → what's next | Always answer "so what?" |
| **Pacing** | Faster than Fer Griego, matches energy of news content | Match pacing to content type |

#### Zauey Hook Formula:
```
[Breaking news/announcement] + [Why you should care]
↓
"[Company] acaba de [hacer X] — esto es lo que significa"
```

#### Script Structure (Zauey News Format):
1. **Hook** (0-3s): The headline, stated dramatically
2. **The Facts** (3-20s): What actually happened, simplified
3. **The Context** (20-35s): Why this matters in the bigger picture
4. **Your Take** (35-50s): Predictions, opinions, what to watch
5. **CTA** (50-60s): Follow for more / comment your thoughts

#### Zauey "Hot Rounds" Series Structure:
1. **Intro** (0-3s): "Las startups más interesantes de esta semana"
2. **Startup 1** (3-18s): Name, raise, problem, solution, traction
3. **Startup 2** (18-33s): Same structure
4. **Startup 3** (33-48s): Same structure
5. **Wrap + CTA** (48-55s): "Y eso es todo por esta semana"

### AskCatGPT (@askcatgpt)
**Category:** AI Culture & Commentary

#### What makes her effective:
| Element | Observation | Takeaway for Luis |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Conversational tone** | Feels like a friend explaining, not a teacher lecturing | Speak *to* the audience, not *at* them |

#### AskCatGPT Hook Formulas:
```
[Contrarian statement] + [Promise to explain]
↓
"Hot take: [controversial opinion]. Déjame explicar."

[Personal story setup] + [Universal lesson]
↓
"En 2022 me pasó [X]... y esto cambió cómo pienso sobre [Y]"

[Common misconception] + [Reframe]
↓
"Todos dicen que IA [hace X]. Están equivocados."
```

#### Script Structure (AskCatGPT Story Format):

2. **Story/Context** (5-30s): The journey, the problem, the situation
3. **Turning Point** (30-40s): What changed, what you learned
4. **Lesson/Reframe** (40-50s): The insight the audience can apply
5. **Empowerment CTA** (50-60s): "Tú también puedes hacerlo"

## 5. Content Format Summary

### Primary Format: Talking Head + Text Overlays
- **Duration**: 45-90 seconds
- **Framing**: Centered or rule-of-thirds, good lighting
- **Text**: Bold hook text on screen for first 3 seconds
- **B-roll/Graphics**: Use when explaining mechanisms or showing examples

### Production Notes
- Start with high-quality talking head (like Fer Griego or Jose Ferrer)
- Add screenshots/graphics when demonstrating (like cyberjasbytes)
- Scale production complexity based on content type:
  - News updates → simpler production
  - Tutorials/How-tos → more visual aids
  - Stories → can be pure talking head
